## 1. Module Overview & Proposal Objectives

This module automates the billing process, computes mathematically accurate grand totals and taxes, handles cash received and change due (remaining balance), and renders clean printable receipts.

### Original Proposal Objectives:
- Generate bills automatically from selected items.
- Calculate totals and payments accurately.
- Handle received amounts and remaining balances.
- Provide a printable invoice or receipt.
